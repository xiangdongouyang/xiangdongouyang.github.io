Our CNC Machining Process for Automotive Prototype Parts

2026-05-04 · 6 min read · X.d Prototype Engineering Team

Overseas buyers cannot walk through our shop floor, so here is the next best thing: the exact workflow every CNC prototype order follows at X.d Prototype, from first email to delivered parts.

Step 1 - Quote and DFM (within 24 hours)

You send drawings (STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF or PDF) with quantity. We review manufacturability, recommend material and process options, and return a quotation with a free DFM analysis. For EV cooling parts this includes an airtightness review — sealing faces, weld joint design and test pressure.

Step 2 - CAM and fixturing

Approved orders go to CAM programming and fixture design. Fixtures are designed in-house so machining datums match inspection datums, and the same fixture family usually serves future repeat batches — making your second order faster and cheaper than your first.

Step 3 - Machining

Milling, turning, drilling and tapping run under controlled parameters for aluminum 6061/6063/AlSi12Fe and other materials. Complex cavity work for cooling channels is sequenced before any welding operation. Critical dimensions target ±0.02 mm.

Step 4 - Surface treatment

Sandblasting, anodizing and brushing happen in controlled batches, with sealing faces masked where coating thickness would break a tolerance or joint spec.

Step 5 - Inspection and testing

CMM inspection covers critical dimensions. Pressure and sealing parts additionally get 100% airtight testing. Reports accompany the shipment — ask for them and they are standard, not an upsell.

Step 6 - Packing and delivery

Parts are individually protected, packed and express-shipped. Typical door-to-door time to the USA and Germany is a few working days, and tracking is shared as soon as the parcel moves.

What we need from you

The fastest quotes come with: 3D model, 2D drawing with tolerances, quantity, target date, material and surface treatment preferences. That is five lines of information that save days of back-and-forth.
